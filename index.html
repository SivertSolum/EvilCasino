HOW TO PLAY

THE RUN

Climb 7 floors. Beat Satan on the last one.

Pick a path on the map: fights, shops, events, rests.

Finish the floor contract, then beat the boss. Skip it and your soul is forfeit anyway.

Spend cash on bets and shops. Hit $0 and it's over.

THE HAND

Beat the dealer's total without going over 21.

Count face cards as 10, Aces as 1 or 11.

HIT takes a card. STAND keeps your hand.

DOUBLE doubles the bet for exactly one card.

SPLIT turns a pair into two hands.

THE CATCH

Bosses bend the rules. Theirs is pinned to the table.

The dealer may or may not cheat. Probably will.
